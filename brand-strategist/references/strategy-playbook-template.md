# Brand Strategy Playbook Template

Use this structure for full engagements. Omit irrelevant sections for focused work. Replace prompts with decisions and evidence; do not present placeholders as a finished strategy.

## 2. Evidence and diagnosis

- State sources, dates, known facts, user reports, assumptions, and important unknowns.
- Explain up to three priority problems using evidence, cause, business consequence, and competing explanations.
- Identify useful assets and existing equity to preserve.
- State what new evidence would change the diagnosis.

## 3. Strategic choice

| Route | Audience/situation | Promise and mechanism | Proof | Feasibility | Trade-offs | Judgment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Recommend one direction. Explain exclusions, required capabilities, and conditions that would overturn the decision. Avoid numerical scoring unless the basis is defined.

## 4. Brand foundation

- Practical role or purpose.
- Future ambition.
- Promise.
- Behavior-based principles.
- Personality expressed through behaviors and boundaries.
- Associations connected to experiences and proof.
- Architecture or naming decisions only when relevant.

## 6. Identity direction

- Desired perception shift and audience context.
- Recognizable assets to retain.
- Creative territories with rationale and trade-offs.
- Recommended concept and practical applications.
- Typography, color, composition, imagery, graphics, and motion roles.
- Distinctive assets to repeat consistently.
- Accessibility, licensing, and production items requiring verification.
- Designer handoff and deliverable specifications.

## 7. Experience and implementation

| Priority touchpoint | Customer expectation | Message/proof | Operational behavior | Owner role | Definition of done |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Action | Priority | Dependency | Effort band | Owner role | Completion evidence | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Sequence work around resources. Separate operational dependencies from publishable claims. Include migration and rollback criteria when rebranding.

## 8. Validation and governance

- Separate approved choices from provisional hypotheses.
- Identify the riskiest assumptions and appropriate tests.
- Set decision rules before results; explain the basis.
- Separate outputs, leading indicators, and business outcomes.
- Define review triggers and responsibility for future updates.
- State research and attribution limitations.

## 9. Evidence appendix

Include source references, dates, evidence status, contradictions, and interview or test limitations. Distinguish real quotations from paraphrases and synthetic examples. Protect private information.
