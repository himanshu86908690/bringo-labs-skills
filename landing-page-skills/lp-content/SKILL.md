# Landing page content

1. Read the brief, traffic message, approved claims, and existing copy. Preserve the approved offer and CTA on focused edits. Draft from confirmed facts and flag decisions that affect publication.
2. Map each major buyer question to one section, an answer, and evidence. Lead with the offer, intended buyer, concrete value, and next action. Match the incoming ad or outreach promise and make the first CTA easy to find.
3. Use the buyer’s language. Put approved proof near the claim it supports. Include price, fit, constraints, and terms where they matter. Scan the page by headings and remove repeated claims, filler, and decorative labels.
4. Write the full action path. Include CTA, field labels and helper text, errors, recovery, confirmation, and what the visitor receives or can expect next. Match the actual follow-up process.
5. Trace material claims, testimonials, numbers, and limitations to evidence and usage permission. Keep placeholders and unsupported claims out of publishable copy. Return copy in page order with asset dependencies and any unresolved decisions.

## Draft and edit by evidence

For a new page, use the traffic promise and buyer language to choose the lead message. For a rewrite, identify the issue in the current copy before replacing it. For a point edit, change the requested section and check nearby transitions and CTA consistency. Read the headings and CTAs alone to verify the sequence. For a consequential claim that cannot be used, record what evidence and permission would make it publishable.
