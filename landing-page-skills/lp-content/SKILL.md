# Landing page content

1. Read the brief, traffic message, approved claims, and existing copy. For a rewrite, identify the problem first. For a point edit, preserve the offer and CTA and check adjacent transitions. Draft from confirmed facts and flag decisions that affect publication.
2. Map each major buyer question to one section, an answer, and evidence. Lead with the offer, intended buyer, concrete value, and next action. Match the incoming ad or outreach promise and make the first CTA easy to find.
3. Use the buyer’s language. Put approved proof near the claim it supports. Include price, fit, constraints, and terms where they matter. Read headings and CTAs in sequence. Cut repeated claims and labels that do not help visitors decide or act.
4. Write the full action path. Include CTA, field labels and helper text, errors, recovery, confirmation, and what the visitor receives or can expect next. Check the primary action, destination, and acceptance signal before finalizing the CTA and confirmation. If the intended integration is unavailable, use an agreed fallback and describe exactly what the visitor must do. For an email draft, say the visitor must send it. Promise a booking only when the booking path is available.
5. Trace material claims, testimonials, numbers, and limitations to evidence and usage permission. Keep placeholders and unsupported claims out of publishable copy. Record what evidence or permission would support an excluded claim. Return copy in page order with asset dependencies and any unresolved decisions.
